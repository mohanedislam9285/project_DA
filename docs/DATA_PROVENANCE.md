- Dataset: *Air Quality - Mediterranean Basin (2016-2026)*, Kaggle user Sarah Ameziane, version 1.
- Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sarahameziane/air-quality-mediterranean-basin-20162026
- Retrieval metadata date: 2026-11-09 (`data/manifest.yaml`). The live Kaggle API metadata was also captured on 2026-11-11 in `data/provenance/kaggle_dataset_metadata_2026-11-11.json`; it reports version 1, last updated 2026-04-20, and license label “Other (specified in description)”.
- The publisher's description identifies OpenAQ CC BY 4.0, Copernicus open-access products, and NASA open data, and says to check each source's terms. It describes a date plus nearest-latitude/longitude GEE join with an approximate 0.25-degree threshold. These statements are retained in the metadata snapshot. They do not establish redistribution rights for the compiled CSV or prove the exact ETL code used.
- Rights assessment checked on 2026-11-11: the Kaggle license label does not itself identify a standard redistribution license, and the description does not provide a compiled-bundle license or component-by-component attribution map. OpenAQ terms/license metadata are provider-specific. The Copernicus Sentinel legal notice allows lawful reuse with source notice; Copernicus service products also require attribution and products sourced from third parties can carry separate terms. Because the bundle has no field-to-upstream-product lineage, those component terms cannot establish the rights or required attribution for the compiled CSV. NASA's general data policy does not clear unidentified third-party contributions. The exact OpenAQ source records and NASA products are likewise not mapped. Do not claim the bundle is cleared for redistribution until its rights and attribution are confirmed. - Source file checksums are in `data/manifest.yaml`.
- Active input is `data/raw/extracted/combined_gee_pm25.csv`, a precombined PM2.5/GEE table. The publisher describes a nearest-coordinate join, and `audit_upstream_join.py` compares it row by row to the supplied component files. Of 41,718 combined rows, all 41,718 match a PM row on date, station name and exact coordinates, and all 41,718 match a GEE row on `id/date`; in every combined row the PM and GEE coordinates coincide (0 km distance) and the PM value agrees within 3.6e-15 µg/m³. The corresponding station/date/coordinate and GEE id/date keys are unique in the supplied files.
- Across the full PM source (88,041 rows), 41,718 rows (47.38%) occur in the combined bundle; 46,323 do not. Across GEE (164,208 rows), 41,718 rows (25.41%) occur in the bundle; 122,490 do not. Recomputing nearest same-day GEE locations under a great-circle interpretation of the stated 0.25-degree radius gives 77,569 PM rows with a same-day GEE record, 53,792 whose nearest row is within the approximate 27.8 km threshold, and 12,074 within-threshold PM rows absent from the combined bundle. Another 23,777 same-day rows are beyond the threshold and 10,472 have no same-day GEE rows. There are 191 nearest-distance ties within 1 m among all PM source rows, but none among the combined rows; 40,631 combined rows have multiple GEE locations inside the threshold even though the selected exact-coordinate nearest row is unique. Thus the bundle correspondences are reproducible, while its full inclusion rule and tie handling for excluded records remain unverified. Machine-readable details and row-level match tables are in `results/16/provenance_audit/`.
