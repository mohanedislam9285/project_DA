# Methodology and Experiment State

## Forecast task
At station s and date t, use observations through the end of t to predict PM2.5 at t+1. Lookback is seven consecutive UTC daily rows. Sequences remain within station and reject date gaps. Exogenous satellite/weather/pollutant variables are excluded because availability at forecast origin is undocumented. The reference feature set is four calendar cycles, PM2.5 lag 1, and rolling means over 3 and 7 days. A nested four-family experiment compared calendar/rolling variants on identical outer-fold targets. The final development artifact was selected separately on one chronological inner split and uses lag-1 only; it must not be presented as independently evaluated.

## Leakage control and validation
The median imputer and standard scaler are fit on training sequences only. Temporal folds are expanding date blocks with a one-day embargo; all stations on each date stay together. Spatial folds use GroupKFold over station IDs. Spatial validation tests held-out station targets conditional on historical observations for those stations; it is not a cold-start station test and not future-time validation. The development period ends before 2024-12-08.

## CNN-LSTM and tuning
The network is causal Conv1D, normalization, ReLU, dropout, optional max pooling, LSTM, dense ReLU and scalar output. Pooling can be disabled to preserve all input steps; the established configuration still uses pool size 2. A nested Optuna-TPE study completed 50 trials in each of five temporal outer folds, with seeded fold-specific selection by inner chronological-validation RMSE. The outer validation was scored once after selection. Early stopping and learning-rate scheduling operate only on inner validation. Trial parameters, scores, epochs and runtimes are retained. The final development checkpoint was saved and reloaded from a configuration selected only using inner-fold evidence; it does not replace or represent the frozen historical model.

## Development results
The original finite-grid temporal fold means are RMSE 8.455 (SD 6.161), MAE 3.911 (SD .957), R2 .106 (SD .151), based on 2,867 OOF predictions. Spatial grouped means are RMSE 7.962 (SD 4.143), MAE 3.292 (SD .353), R2 .212 (SD .138), based on 3,349 predictions. A fixed-config diagnostic scored temporal, spatial, and random CV; its mean RMSEs are 8.301, 7.939, and 8.091 respectively. Random-minus-temporal is −0.209 and random-minus-spatial is +0.152; the random split did not produce a lower error than temporal in this run. Intervals resample fold scores and are descriptive only because five folds are dependent and few.

## Diagnostics
Seeds 42/43/44, training-size fractions 10/25/50/75/100%, parameter sensitivity, available-feature ablations, a log1p-target ablation, a missingness-indicator ablation, validation-channel permutation importance, station-month pollutant missingness, uninterrupted-segment ACF/PACF and stationarity tests, local OOF cases, and residual analyses are saved under `results/16/`. Sixty-three stations have a development segment of at least 14 daily observations for ACF/PACF; six have at least 30 observations for formal tests. The required exogenous feature ablations remain blocked because weather, trace-gas and AOD availability at forecast origin is unverified. A sample-weighted MSE experiment used weights 1x for PM2.5 ≤25, 2x for 25-50, and 4x above 50; high-tail error traded off against low-range and overall RMSE. Only seven OOF targets exceeded 50 µg/m³, so tail results are unstable. For the finite-grid OOF arrays, zero-excluded pooled MAPE is 50.14% temporal (n=2,867) and 42.13% spatial (n=3,349). All cleaned targets are positive, but near-zero denominators still make MAPE unstable; RMSE and MAE are primary. Spatial grouped OOF pooled scores are RMSE 8.700, MAE 3.287 and R² .154; they test held-out stations conditional on historical sequences, not future-period transfer.

## Corrected naive baselines
Persistence uses the observed PM2.5 value at the sequence forecast origin t, not the t+1 target. On the five temporal folds, mean fold RMSE was 8.771 µg/m³ for persistence and 10.798 for weekly seasonal naive. The Optuna CNN-LSTM mean was 8.520; its paired fold difference from persistence was not statistically significant after Holm correction. Pooled OOF metrics differ from fold means and are reported separately.

## Frozen historical estimate
Saved future metrics: RMSE 6.027207, MAE 4.024034, R2 0.239368 on 1,716 predictions. These are historical frozen evidence; no future labels were read or metrics recomputed. A development-only reconstruction using the recorded candidate and epoch reproduces all saved predictions (max absolute difference 7.1e-15), establishing output equivalence but not byte identity of the missing original checkpoint.

## Further development-only experiments
Four architecture variants (direct/residual scalar head; max-pooling on/off) were fitted on identical outer folds and scored on the same 2,867 OOF targets. Direct/no-pooling had mean-fold RMSE 7.901 and MAE 3.382, versus reference 8.520/3.978; however its exact Wilcoxon comparison with persistence is p=1.00, Holm p=1.00, and the paired cluster interval includes zero. Residual/no-pooling had 8.660/3.141 (paired p=.125, Holm=.75). Nested inner-selected architectures had 9.171 mean-fold RMSE. These mixed results are sensitivity evidence, not a reliable improvement.

A nested 3-vs-7-day history experiment restricted training and validation to identical sample IDs. On 2,867 OOF targets, pooled RMSE is 9.579 vs 9.571 and MAE is 3.899 vs 3.927; inner validation chose seven days on four folds. No meaningful advantage is established. Fourteen-day lookback is too sparse for comparable five-fold validation: only 505 sequences overall, including validation folds of 45, 2, 8, 145 and 255. Results and fitted artifacts are under `results/16/lookback_experiment/` and `results/16/architecture_experiment/`.

The original reference shows train-to-validation RMSE gaps of 1.728 to 5.792 in fold 1 and 4.043 to 18.877 in fold 3, consistent with overfitting in those periods. Fold 4 has the reverse pattern (8.828 train; 5.106 validation), evidence that folds differ in target difficulty and distribution. The predictions are compressed (OOF prediction SD 3.37 vs target SD 9.96); the >=50 band contains seven samples and has CNN-LSTM MAE 143.43 vs persistence 127.18. These results indicate weak event-tail handling despite lower average fold RMSE.


## Nested feature-family follow-up and final development artifact
Four feature families were compared using the same 2,867 outer temporal targets and existing fold hyperparameters. Inner selection used chronological validation; it varied by fold (full twice, lag-only twice, lag + rolling-3 once). The selected procedure averaged fold RMSE 8.378 and MAE 3.784; versus persistence, exact Wilcoxon RMSE p=.813 and Holm-adjusted p=1.00. The best fixed family (no calendar + lag-1 + rolling-3) averaged 7.894 RMSE / 3.396 MAE; exact fold test versus reference p=.063, Holm p=.313 across five feature procedures. It did not establish a reliable improvement over persistence. See `results/16/feature_family_experiment/` and `experiment_metadata.json`.

The latest full-development artifact selected lag-only on a 2,275/1,059 chronological train/validation split and then refit on 3,349 development sequences for 27 epochs. Its inner validation score is selection evidence only, not an independent test score. Model, preprocessor, config, seed, history, metadata and in-sample smoke predictions are saved under `results/16/`; no holdout labels or metrics were used.


## Additional input-timing and cleaning checks (2026-11-12)
A development-only cleaning audit found zero invalid PM2.5 values under the configured 0&lt;y&lt;500 rule, zero duplicate station-day keys, and zero invalid coordinates. A station-specific upper fence Q3+3×IQR flagged 352 values among 71 stations with n≥30. All flags are retained for review because statistical extremeness alone does not establish instrument error. Development counts were 90 values &gt;50, 36 &gt;100 and 11 &gt;250 µg/m³. Audit outputs are in `results/16/cleaning_quality_audit/`.

One-day-lagged meteorological/pollutant/AOD channels did not improve the target-history-only CNN-LSTM on identical temporal folds. The inner-selected mixture had pooled RMSE 9.356 and MAE 3.645 versus target-history 9.322/3.462; exact paired RMSE versus persistence p=.813. Source delivery latency remains unknown, so these are conditional research diagnostics. Same-origin neighbor PM means within 25/50/100 km also raised pooled RMSE to 9.361/9.439/9.408 versus 9.322 target-history. Neither expansion is enabled in the finalized model.
